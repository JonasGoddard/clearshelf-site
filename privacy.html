Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

This policy explains how the ClearShelf app for iPhone and iPad ("ClearShelf," "the app") handles information. ClearShelf is published by Jonas Goddard ("we," "us"). If you have a question, email clearshelf.support@gmail.com.

The short version: ClearShelf has no account and no servers of its own. Your groceries, saved recalls, and settings stay on your device. To work, the app asks public sources (the FDA, the USDA, and Open Food Facts) for recall and product information. The free plan shows non-personalized Google ads; ClearShelf Pro removes them. We never sell your information.

Information that stays on your device

These are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. We can't see them:

  • Groceries you scan or type into My Groceries, your shopping list, and saved recalls.
  • Your settings, such as your state, allergens, food types, alert choices, and app color.
  • Package photos you choose with the system photo picker. They aren't uploaded.
  • Camera images while you scan barcodes. They're processed on the device and aren't saved or uploaded.

Like other app data, this information may be included in device backups (for example, iCloud Backup), depending on your device settings. Deleting the app deletes it from your device.

Information sent to public data sources

ClearShelf has no servers of its own; it connects directly to these services. Like any website you visit, they receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address.

  • U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA): the app downloads recall data from openFDA and fda.gov. When you check a barcode, its digits are sent to openFDA. The words you search in Recall History are sent to openFDA too.
  • U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA FSIS): the app downloads recalls and public health alerts.
  • Open Food Facts: barcodes you scan into My Groceries are sent to Open Food Facts to look up product names, brands, and photos. When a recall lists a single clearly labeled barcode, that barcode may also be sent to find a product photo. Only the barcode is sent, along with the app's name and version.

These organizations handle information under their own privacy policies.

Ads (free plan only)

The free plan shows a small banner at the bottom of Settings and, now and then, a full-screen ad when you go back to the Recalls list after reading a few recalls. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. ClearShelf Pro removes all ads, and with Pro the ad software never starts.

  • Never personalized. ClearShelf asks Google to show only non-personalized ads and to use restricted data processing. Non-personalized ads are based on general context, not on a profile of you.
  • No tracking. ClearShelf never asks permission to track you and doesn't use your device's advertising identifier.
  • What Google receives. To show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud, Google's software receives information such as your IP address, approximate location (estimated from your IP address), device and app details, a device identifier used only within apps from the same publisher, and which ads you see and tap. Google may also collect diagnostics such as crash and performance data.
  • What Google doesn't receive. Your groceries, saved recalls, searches, scans, and photos are never shared with Google.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Notifications

If you turn on alerts, the app checks for new recalls in the background and decides on your device whether to notify you. We don't run a notification server, and we don't receive your alert settings.

Purchases

ClearShelf Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never receive your payment details. The app checks with Apple whether Pro is unlocked on your Apple Account.

Support emails and crash reports

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you choose to include (the app's Contact support option adds the app version, build, iOS version, and device type). We use it only to answer you and keep it only as long as needed for that. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't use analytics software or build profiles of you.
  • We don't require an account, name, email, or phone number to use the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Ad privacy choices: where state privacy law gives you choices (for example, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information or targeted advertising), the app shows Settings → Privacy choices. ClearShelf Pro turns ads off entirely.
  • Information on your device: you can edit or delete your groceries and settings in the app at any time, or delete the app to remove everything.
  • Requests to us: because we don't keep your app data, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. You can still email us with any privacy request, including requests under state privacy laws such as those in California, and we'll respond within the time those laws require. We won't treat you differently for making a request.
  • Information held by Google: see Google's controls at myadcenter.google.com.

Children

ClearShelf is a general-audience app and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Where ClearShelf is offered

ClearShelf is intended for people in the United States, and its recall information covers U.S. agencies.

Security

The app connects to the services above over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Information on your device is protected by your device's own security, such as your passcode.

Changes to this policy

If we change how ClearShelf handles information, we'll update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. For significant changes, we'll also mention them in the app's App Store release notes.
